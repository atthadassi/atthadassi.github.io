# About Bhante Atthadassi

Bhante Atthadassi is a Theravada monk dedicated to the study, practice, and dissemination of the Dhamma. Originally from Nepal, he has spent over a decade immersing himself in the monastic traditions of Southeast Asia, focusing on the preservation and practical application of the Buddhist scriptures.

## Monastic Journey

Bhante’s path into the monkhood began in 2015 when he was ordained as a *Samanera* (novice monk) at Panditarama in Myanmar. Seeking to deepen his commitment, he later moved to the Pa-Auk Forest Monastery, where he received higher ordination (*Upasampada*) in 2017 in the presence of thirty-three monks.

Throughout his formative years, he had the privilege of studying under esteemed teachers, including:
* **Pa-Auk Sayadaw**
* **Sayadaw Chandima**
* **Sayadaw U Kovida**

## Academic and Spiritual Focus

As a practitioner within the Theravada tradition, Bhante’s intellectual and spiritual life is centered on the *Tipitaka* (The Three Baskets). His studies encompass:

* **Vinaya Pitaka:** Exploring the ethical framework and code of conduct that sustains the monastic and lay communities.
* **Sutta Pitaka:** Analyzing the discourses and techniques taught by the Buddha to help individuals perceive the true nature of reality.
* **Abhidhamma Pitaka:** Delving into the precise, philosophical analysis of mental and physical phenomena.

In his personal practice, Bhante emphasizes a refined approach to mindfulness—one that prioritizes memory and the active maintenance of wholesome qualities to cultivate a clear and peaceful mind.

## Vision for this Space

Currently residing in a secluded environment dedicated to meditation and Dhamma study, Bhante seeks to bridge the gap between ancient wisdom and the modern world. This website serves as a digital repository for his insights, offering a clear and accessible window into the teachings of the Buddha for practitioners and seekers worldwide.
